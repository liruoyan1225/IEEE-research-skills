# Advisor-style profile reference

## Purpose and boundary

Distil repeatable, observable conventions from user-provided advisor papers and annotations. Do not claim identity-level imitation and do not reuse distinctive language from any source paper.

## Candidate-rule method

For each candidate rule, record:

1. source file and precise location;
2. observed pattern and contrast/example count;
3. scope: paragraph logic, wording, equation layout, figures, citations, or review preference;
4. safe generalized rule;
5. status: proposed, confirmed, rejected, or superseded.

## Default profile requirements

- Prefer technically specific subjects and verbs over generic framing.
- Make paragraph function explicit: premise, prior work, limitation, response, or implication.
- Introduce architecture, variables and abbreviations before relying on them.
- Define whether a displayed equation is essential; keep simple substitutions and definitions inline when that improves readability.
- Tie comparison statements and limitations to specific cited work; avoid unsupported absence claims.
- Treat equation layout, symbol definitions, numerical precision, baseline definition, and figure readability as technical communication—not cosmetic afterthoughts.

These are initial guardrails, not a substitute for a confirmed profile record.

## Update discipline

New corpus material may generate proposed rules only. Present the additions and their evidence to the user. Promote them to `吕老师风格档案.md` only after explicit confirmation.
